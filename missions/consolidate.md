# Mission: consolidate reference knowledge

## Objective

Make existing knowledge easier to find, interpret, and maintain. Resolve avoidable duplication without erasing valid disagreement, provenance, or the history behind decisions.

## Context and authority

Read the brain's entry point, governance, knowledge model, retrieval guidance, relevant domain index, and prior consolidation receipt. Start from a bounded domain, user request, or documented problem. Do not load or rewrite the entire brain by default.

Use the configured autonomy policy. Routine editorial consolidation may be authorized while deleting history, broad restructuring, or declaring a disputed position authoritative may require another action path.

## Working brief

- Find the notes that compete to describe the same subject. Determine whether they are duplicates, different scopes, a current version and its history, or genuinely conflicting accounts.
- Establish or improve a clear reference note. Preserve the sources supporting its claims and distinguish fact, interpretation, proposal, and decision.
- Prefer a focused summary with links to evidence over several copied summaries that can drift independently.
- Preserve decision rationale, alternatives, scope, effective dates, and exceptions when they remain useful. A shorter note is not better if its meaning depends on context that disappeared.
- Where one claim replaces another, identify the replacement and its effective scope. Keep the older account accessible as history when policy allows.
- Repair affected links, indexes, and context packs within the mission's authorized scope. Respect agent environments that only support ordinary Markdown links.
- Leave unsupported or contested claims visible as such. Do not choose a winner solely because a note was edited more recently or written by a more confident contributor.

Consolidation does not create permission to collect new sources or broaden visibility. Follow the existing retention policy; do not move restricted material into a more widely shared note.

## Completion evidence

The run receipt names the domain examined, notes changed, duplicates resolved, links or summaries repaired, and disagreements left open. A reader should be able to locate the current reference and trace important assertions to evidence.

Check the changed notes as a future agent would: follow their entry links and verify that the useful context is still reachable. Do not claim complete coverage of domains you did not inspect.

## Stop and recover

Work within a time or note budget. When a larger restructuring is needed, record the proposed scope and dependencies rather than expanding the task silently. Preserve a reversible edit history using the private brain's existing mechanism.

If two agents may edit the same note, read the current version before writing, detect competing edits, and reconcile meaning instead of overwriting either contribution. Escalate conflicts under policy or leave a recoverable pending item.

See [the knowledge model](../docs/knowledge-model.md) and [continuity](../docs/continuity.md) for freshness and meaning preservation.
